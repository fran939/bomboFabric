# BomboAddons - Features Overview

**BomboAddons** is a high-performance Fabric client mod built for modern Minecraft (26.2), offering a comprehensive suite of Quality-of-Life, HUD, media integration, and utility features.

---

## 🎵 Spotify & Media Integration
- **Spotify HUD:** Floating overlay displaying currently playing track, artist name, dynamic progress bar, and clickable media controls (Previous, Play/Pause, Next).
- **Synchronized Lyrics HUD:** Real-time on-screen lyrics synchronized to playback with word-by-word and character-by-character karaoke wipe animations.
- **Embedded Album Artwork:** Displays real song album covers with automatic fallback to sleek dark vinyl icons.
- **Dynamic & Custom Color Themes:** Automatically tints lyrics accent colors to match album artwork, or choose from custom palette presets.
- **Interactive Lyrics Viewer (`/b lyrics`):** Fullscreen lyrics viewer with seek-on-click navigation and millisecond delay offset tuning per song.

---

## 🎙️ Discord Voice & IPC Integration
- **Discord Voice Call HUD:** Real-time in-game voice overlay displaying participants, speaking indicators, deafen/mute icons, and channel name.
- **Live Stream Indicators:** Visual `[LIVE]` badges identifying users actively screen sharing in the Discord call.
- **In-Game Voice Controls:** Toggle your microphone or deafen status directly from Minecraft (`/b mute`, `/b deafen`, `/b unmute`, `/b undeafen`).
- **Participant Muting:** Click participants in the HUD or use `/b mute <user>` to mute individuals locally.
- **Bridge Chat & Remote Admin (`/cmd`):** Synchronizes chat between Discord and Minecraft, with secure remote command dispatch for server administrators.

---

## 📺 Zero-Lag Screensharing & Streaming
- **Built-in Live Streaming:** Stream gameplay directly to the web dashboard at true 60 FPS in 720p, 1080p, or 1440p.
- **Multi-Monitor Display Capture:** Switch streaming sources seamlessly between Minecraft and physical desktop monitors (`/ss 1`, `/ss 2`, `/ss mc`).
- **Web Dashboard:** Centralized viewer to watch live streams from any browser with real-time status and latency stats.

---

## 🎒 Storage & Inventory Management
- **Storage Overlay:** Clean visual overlay displaying contents of backpacks and ender chests without opening separate inventories.
- **Custom Backpack Names:** Name and color-code individual storage containers for fast navigation.
- **Custom Inventory Buttons:** Configurable shortcut buttons on your inventory screen to quickly execute frequently used commands.
- **Custom Slot Swapping:** Instant item swapping with hotkeys to swap designated items to your active hand.

---

## 👤 Profile Viewer & Networth Breakdown (`/b pv`)
- **Full Player Profile Viewer:** Inspect any player's gear, armor, weapons, accessories, and pet loadouts.
- **Networth Breakdown:** Comprehensive coin valuation of inventories, wardrobe, vaults, and storage.
- **Wardrobe & Storage Inspection:** Browse through remote players' wardrobe sets and backpack contents.

---

## 🌾 SkyBlock & Garden Utilities
- **Hoppity Egg Finder:** Instant detection and waypoint indicators for spawned Hoppity eggs across islands.
- **Pest ESP & Highlights:** Outlines and highlights pests in the Garden with configurable dye colors.
- **Greenhouse Profit Calculator (`/gp`):** Calculates profit and crop margins in the Greenhouse.
- **Dungeon & Catacombs Tools:** Dungeon map room coloring, starred mob highlights, key tracers, and quick floor join shortcuts (`/f1`-`/f7`, `/m1`-`/m7`).

---

## ⚙️ Customization & System Utilities
- **Custom Keybinds & Aliases:** Map custom key combinations to chat messages, commands, or toggles.
- **Feature & Category Organizer (`/b order`):** Drag-and-drop screen to customize the order and categorization of settings in the configuration menu.
- **In-Game Changelog (`/b changelog`):** Browse recent updates, new features, and bug fixes directly in-game.
- **Full HUD Editor (`/b hud`):** Reposition and resize any HUD element with drag-and-drop handles and snapping guides.
